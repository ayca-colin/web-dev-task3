Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue started in 2010. A group of neighbors wanted to help stray dogs and cats. Today, we are a large community nonprofit saving hundreds of animals every year.

Core Values

  • Hope for every animal
  • Trust with our community
  • Action to save lives
